Seven clues

Seven clues drop on @chrisberlin at odd hours, marked ⌖. Each one describes the witness. Each lives 60 minutes, then it's gone forever. Use them to cross faces off the crowd below.

Name the witness

When you know, make your guess. One guess per player, sealed so no one can see it or copy it. It counts if you caught at least 3 of the 7 clues. Every guess stays sealed until the last clue's hour ends. Then the first right answer wins the blind bounty.

One guess

What you need. A seat costs nothing. To catch a clue or make your guess you send a shielded Zcash message from a Zcash wallet such as Zashi or Zingo. Each one carries a tiny amount, 0.0001 ZEC, plus your wallet's network fee. That's what makes it private and timestamped by the blockchain.

Catching a clue. Every clue carries a code. Send that code within the hour to prove you were there. Your catches show on the board.

Shielded or visible

Zcash lets you choose what the chain reveals. A shielded payment is real and valid, but it doesn't say who sent it.

Here it works the same way. Shielded, your face is in the crowd, but no one can tell whose it is. Your handle is never shown or saved.

Taking a seat needs no wallet and costs nothing. Proving takes one tiny shielded payment, because that is what makes a proof private.

Catch the clues

Each clue on X carries a code. Seal the code inside a shielded Zcash message and send it before the hour runs out. The blockchain is the clock: the block your message lands in decides. Turn on notifications for @chrisberlin.

The game begins when the doors close on Halloween. Take your seat before then. Watch for ⌖.
